# PLACEHOLDER triage notes (for development only)

These are short, team-written stand-ins so the retrieval pipeline has something
to search. They are NOT a clinical source. Replace them with summaries of real,
citable guidelines (e.g. CDC, NIH, AHA public pages) before any demo, and put
the source URL in each section.

Format the pipeline understands: one "## " heading per complaint, then lines
starting with "Consider:", "Red flags:", "Workup:", "Ask:" (items separated by ";").

## Chest pain in adults
Consider: Acute coronary syndrome; Gastroesophageal reflux; Musculoskeletal chest wall pain; Pulmonary embolism
Red flags: Pain radiating to arm or jaw; Diaphoresis; Shortness of breath at rest; Syncope; Hypotension
Workup: 12-lead ECG; Troponin; Vital signs including oxygen saturation
Ask: Onset and duration of pain; Exertional or at rest; Prior cardiac history
Risk factors that raise concern include diabetes, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, smoking and older age.

## Shortness of breath and wheezing
Consider: Asthma exacerbation; COPD exacerbation; Pneumonia; Heart failure
Red flags: Inability to speak full sentences; Oxygen saturation below 92 percent; Silent chest; Cyanosis
Workup: Pulse oximetry; Peak flow; Chest X-ray if fever or focal findings
Ask: Rescue inhaler use frequency; Recent infection; Exposure to triggers

## Fever and cough
Consider: Viral upper respiratory infection; Community-acquired pneumonia; Influenza; COVID-19
Red flags: Respiratory rate above 30; Confusion; Low blood pressure; Oxygen saturation below 92 percent
Workup: Temperature and vitals; Respiratory viral testing; Chest X-ray if pneumonia suspected
Ask: Duration of fever; Sick contacts; Vaccination status

## Abdominal pain
Consider: Gastroenteritis; Appendicitis; Cholecystitis; Urinary tract infection
Red flags: Rigid abdomen; Pain migrating to right lower quadrant; Blood in stool or vomit; Pregnancy possibility with pain
Workup: Abdominal exam; Urinalysis; Pregnancy test when applicable; CBC
Ask: Location and migration of pain; Relation to meals; Bowel and urinary changes

## Headache
Consider: Tension-type headache; Migraine; Sinusitis; Hypertensive headache
Red flags: Sudden worst headache of life; Fever with neck stiffness; New neurological deficit; Headache after head injury
Workup: Blood pressure; Neurological exam; Fundoscopic exam
Ask: Onset speed; Pattern compared to usual headaches; Visual changes

## High blood sugar symptoms
Consider: Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus; Diabetic ketoacidosis; Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state; Infection precipitating hyperglycemia
Red flags: Vomiting with high glucose; Confusion or drowsiness; Rapid deep breathing; Fruity breath odor
Workup: Point-of-care glucose; Urine or serum ketones; Basic metabolic panel
Ask: Missed medication doses; Recent illness; Increased thirst or urination
